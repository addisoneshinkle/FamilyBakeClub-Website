Rainbow Cupcakes

These fluffy vanilla cupcakes are perfect for birthdays and celebrations! With colorful swirl frosting and tons of sprinkles, they're guaranteed to bring smiles to everyone at the party. The recipe is beginner-friendly and fun for kids to help with.

⏱ 45 minutes 🧁 12 cupcakes 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Kid-friendly

Quick Info

⏱️ Prep Time 15 minutes
🔥 Bake Time 18 minutes
❄️ Cool Time 30 minutes
🌡️ Temperature 350°F (175°C)
⭐ Difficulty Easy - Beginner
🎂 Best For Birthdays & parties

🥣 Ingredients

Everything you need for perfect cupcakes!

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter (softened)
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs (room temperature)
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup whole milk (room temperature)
  • Food coloring (optional - various colors)
  • Rainbow sprinkles for decorating

👩‍🍳 Instructions

Follow these simple steps for perfect cupcakes every time!

  1. Preheat and Prep: Heat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a 12-cup muffin tin with colorful cupcake liners. This is a great job for kids!
  2. Mix Dry Ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.
  3. Cream Butter & Sugar: In a large bowl, beat softened butter and sugar with an electric mixer until light and fluffy (about 3-4 minutes). The mixture should look pale and creamy.
  4. Add Eggs & Vanilla: Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Mix in vanilla extract until combined.
  5. Combine Everything: Alternate adding the flour mixture and milk to the butter mixture, starting and ending with flour. Mix on low speed until just combined. Don't overmix!
  6. Add Colors (Optional): Divide batter into separate bowls and add different food coloring to each. Spoon colorful batters into liners for a rainbow effect!
  7. Fill Liners: Fill each cupcake liner about 2/3 full. This helps them rise perfectly without overflowing.
  8. Bake: Bake for 16-19 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. The tops should spring back when lightly touched.
  9. Cool Completely: Let cupcakes cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack. Wait until they're completely cool before frosting (this is important!).
  10. Frost & Decorate: Once cool, frost with your favorite buttercream, add swirls, and top with rainbow sprinkles. Get creative!

💡 Baker's Tips & Notes

Room Temperature Matters: Make sure your eggs and milk are at room temperature. This helps create a smoother batter and fluffier cupcakes!

Don't Overmix: Mix the batter until just combined. Overmixing can make your cupcakes dense instead of light and fluffy.

Cooling is Crucial: We learned this the hard way! Let cupcakes cool ALL the way before frosting, or your frosting will melt and slide off.

Storage: Store frosted cupcakes in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days.

Kid Jobs: Kids can help measure dry ingredients, mix colors into batter, fill liners, and decorate with sprinkles!
