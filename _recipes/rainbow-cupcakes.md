---
recipe_name: Rainbow Cupcakes
short_description: Fluffy vanilla cupcakes with swirl frosting and sprinkles.
full_description:
    These fluffy vanilla cupcakes are perfect for birthdays and celebrations! With colorful swirl frosting and tons of
    sprinkles, they're guaranteed to bring smiles to everyone at the party. The recipe is beginner-friendly and fun for
    kids to help with.
tags:

prep_time: 15 minutes
bake_time: 18 minutes
cool_time: 30 minutes
total_time: 45 minutes
quantity: 12 cupcakes
temperature: 350°F (175°C)
difficulty: Easy
best_for: Birthdays & parties
ingredients:
    - 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
    - 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
    - 1/4 teaspoon salt
    - 1/2 cup unsalted butter (softened)
    - 3/4 cup granulated sugar
    - 2 large eggs (room temperature)
    - 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
    - 1/2 cup whole milk (room temperature)
    - Food coloring (optional - various colors)
    - Rainbow sprinkles for decorating
instructions:
    - title: Preheat and Prep
      description:
          Heat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a 12-cup muffin tin with colorful cupcake liners. This is a great job
          for kids!
    - title: Mix Dry Ingredients
      description: In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.
    - title: Cream Butter & Sugar
      description:
          In a large bowl, beat softened butter and sugar with an electric mixer until light and fluffy (about 3-4
          minutes). The mixture should look pale and creamy.
    - title: Add Eggs & Vanilla
      description: Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Mix in vanilla extract until combined.
    - title: Combine Everything
      description:
          Alternate adding the flour mixture and milk to the butter mixture, starting and ending with flour. Mix on low
          speed until just combined. Don't overmix!
    - title: Add Colors (Optional)
      description:
          Divide batter into separate bowls and add different food coloring to each. Spoon colorful batters into liners
          for a rainbow effect!
    - title: Fill Liners
      description: Fill each cupcake liner about 2/3 full. This helps them rise perfectly without overflowing.
    - title: Bake
      description:
          Bake for 16-19 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. The tops should spring back
          when lightly touched.
    - title: Cool Completely
      description:
          Let cupcakes cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack. Wait until they're completely cool
          before frosting (this is important!).
    - title: Frost & Decorate
      description:
          Once cool, frost with your favorite buttercream, add swirls, and top with rainbow sprinkles. Get creative!
tips_and_notes:
    - title: Room Temperature Matters
      description:
          Make sure your eggs and milk are at room temperature. This helps create a smoother batter and fluffier
          cupcakes!
    - title: Don't Overmix
      description:
          Mix the batter until just combined. Overmixing can make your cupcakes dense instead of light and fluffy.
    - title: Cooling is Crucial
      description:
          We learned this the hard way! Let cupcakes cool ALL the way before frosting, or your frosting will melt and
          slide off.
    - title: Storage
      description:
          Store frosted cupcakes in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days, or refrigerate for up to
          5 days.
    - title: Kid Jobs
      description:
          Kids can help measure dry ingredients, mix colors into batter, fill liners, and decorate with sprinkles!
